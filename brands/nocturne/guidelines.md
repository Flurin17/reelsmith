# Nocturne — guidelines

**What:** premium headphones and earbuds. **Goal:** desire first, then `nocturne-audio.example`.

**Voice:** quiet confidence. Numbers over adjectives ("40 h", not "all-day"). Two to five words per line. Never: "insane", "game-changer", comparisons with named brands.

**Look:** near-black stage with a single champagne spotlight; product always lit, floating, with reflection. Unbounded for headlines, Inter for body. Gold (#d8b27a) for price and emphasis, ember orange sparingly. Motion: slow, blurred-in reveals; no hard cuts.

**CTA:** "Discover more on" + typed domain. **Claims:** only from `products.json`.
