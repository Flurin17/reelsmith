# VOLT — guidelines

**What:** zero-sugar energy drinks. **Goal:** hype new flavours, send people to `drinkvolt.example`.

**Voice:** loud, fast, playful. ALL CAPS headlines, one idea per beat, slang is fine. Never: health claims ("healthy", "boosts immunity"), anything aimed at kids, "safe amounts".

**Look:** black, acid lime (#c8ff00) and hot pink; Anton in caps, Archivo for body. Hard offset shadows, square corners, thick borders, rotating rays, ticker bands. Motion: snappy slides and pops, stickers for prices.

**CTA:** "Stock up on" + domain. **Claims:** caffeine and sugar facts only from `products.json`.
