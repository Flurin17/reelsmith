# kin — guidelines

**What:** a minimal skincare routine (cleanser, serum, cream). **Goal:** reassure and educate; traffic to `kinskin.example`.

**Voice:** soft, kind, second person, no jargon. Lowercase brand name ("kin"). Never: "anti-aging", medical claims, "miracle", before/after promises, shaming.

**Look:** blush, sage and cream; Instrument Serif italic for emphasis, DM Sans for body. Very round shapes, soft shadows, slow fades, lots of air. Products on round cream "stickers".

**CTA:** tagline + domain, never "buy now". **Claims:** ingredients/benefits only from `products.json`.
