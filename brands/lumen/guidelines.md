# Lumen — guidelines

**What:** considered objects for the home (lighting, ceramics, scent). **Goal of videos:** inspire, then send people to `lumen-home.example`.

**Voice:** calm, warm, a little literary. Short sentences. Sensory, concrete ("2700 K", "hand-thrown"). Never: hype, urgency, exclamation marks, "must-have".

**Look:** warm paper and a faint grid; serif display (Fraunces) with generous negative space; terracotta for emphasis, ochre highlights, olive as a calm second tone. Soft shadows, near-square corners. Motion: slow mask reveals, nothing bouncy.

**CTA:** invite, don't push: "Explore more on", "Find yours on". **Claims:** only specs from `products.json`.
