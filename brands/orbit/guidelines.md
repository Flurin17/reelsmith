# Orbit — guidelines

**What:** a money app for freelancers (balance, invoices, taxes set aside). **Goal:** app installs via `orbit-money.example`.

**Voice:** clear, reassuring, modern. Plain numbers, no finance jargon. Never: returns/interest promises, "guaranteed", investment advice, fake testimonials.

**Look:** deep indigo with violet/mint/pink gradient mesh, glass cards, Plus Jakarta Sans. The product is the UI: show real-looking screens (balance, income chart, payment notification) inside a phone. Motion: smooth rises, gentle phone tilt.

**CTA:** "Get Orbit free" + typed domain. **Claims:** no numbers that imply guaranteed outcomes; demo figures are illustrative.
