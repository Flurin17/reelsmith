# Short-form craft notes

Defaults that hold across TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Brand guidelines override them.

## Length

| Type                     | Target  | Notes                             |
| ------------------------ | ------- | --------------------------------- |
| Product hit / spotlight  | 9–15 s  | One product, one reason to care.  |
| Explainer / how-to       | 20–35 s | 3–5 beats.                        |
| Buying guide / deep dive | 35–60 s | Only if the topic truly needs it. |

`reelsmith review` warns outside 6–60 s and notes anything over 35 s.

## Hook (first 1–2 seconds)

- Text on screen by 0.8 s (the review checks this) and ≤ 6 words.
- Open with a contrast, a mistake, a number, a price/budget angle, a local angle,
  or a curiosity gap: "Your desk is killing your focus." / "Forty hours. One charge."
- Say and show the same idea; don't make viewers read one thing and hear another.

## Beats

- One idea per scene. If a scene needs "and", split it.
- On-screen words ≤ ~3 per second of scene (the review flags faster).
- Alternate visual weight between scenes (full-bleed color ↔ product ↔ big type) so
  consecutive frames look different.
- Sound on every scene change (swipe/whoosh) and reveals (pop/ding). The templates
  already do this; keep it when you build new ones.

## Captions

Most people watch muted. Narrated templates show word-synced captions from the
voiceover timings; keep them on. Keep captions clear of the bottom 320 px (platform
caption zone) — the review's platform-UI check covers this.

## CTA

- Soft beats hard: point to the domain ("See them all on …") instead of "BUY NOW".
- Last 2–4 seconds; the domain must be fully visible before the end (the review's
  last frame shows it).
- Follow the CTA rules in `brand/guidelines.md` (order, wording, what never to say).

## Claims

Only what the catalog or guidelines state. No invented specs, prices, dates,
availability, or competitor comparisons.
